The Concept

One format. Every city
gets its own season.

Her City [City Name] — Her City Beijing, Her City Shanghai, Her City Chengdu, Her City Xi'an. Same proven structure, a new host and a new city each time.

Episodes / Season

5 × 10 minutes

50 minutes of story per city, built to release as a complete season or roll out episode by episode.

Host Format

One "Tour Girl," Locked Per City

A single consistent animated host, styled to local culture and dialect cues, carried across all five episodes on a locked character bible.

Tone

Fun. Real. Emotional.

Practical travel information — pros, cons, hidden spots, daily life — carried through personal narrative moments, not a straight listicle.

Content Mix

What Guidebooks Skip

Must-see spots, local food, transport, safety, cost of living, and foreigner-specific tips, all through the host's own daily life in the city.

Language

Mandarin-Led, Fully Bilingual

Mandarin primary with English subtitles, plus a foreigner-facing cut with English narration for global audiences.

Audience

Chinese & Foreign Viewers

Built to serve domestic Chinese platforms and a global YouTube audience discovering the city for the first time.
